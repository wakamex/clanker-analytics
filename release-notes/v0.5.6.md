clanker-analytics now records Muse Code's quota, and fixes two problems in the per-point prices `pace --json` gives each quota window ([`6b87319`](https://github.com/wakamex/clanker-analytics/commit/6b87319479f8f7a243ef601af4b5ba7e6d23156d)).

- `record-quota` records Muse Code's 5-hour and weekly windows from [clanker-usage](https://github.com/wakamex/clanker-usage), so `pace` reports them and can price them from their completed periods.
- Codex's weekly price on the plus plan took in weeks from the pro plan, because a plain `pro` stopped being a priced plan in model-prices once it covered three tiers. On one host this made the price $18.53 a point instead of $2.64. Plans now compare by family, so `pro` and `pro_200`, or `ultra_100` and `Google AI Ultra`, are one plan, and `pro` and `plus` are two.
- A window whose used points mostly show no local cost now has no price, with `mostly_unaccounted` set, instead of a price from the small measured part. `measured_api_usd_per_quota_percentage_point` keeps that ratio.
